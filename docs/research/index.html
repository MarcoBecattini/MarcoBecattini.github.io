Research

My research investigates how software engineering principles and techniques can be adapted to design and govern LLM-based multi-agent systems. I focus on software architecture, agent coordination and accountability, execution control, reliability, and performance.

LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems

My doctoral research connects agent organisation and execution control with reliability and performance under uncertainty. It includes SALLMA, accountability-based agent cooperation, social agent discovery, and empirical studies of HorseReport and CAPRA.

My thesis is entitled On Reliability and Performance in LLM-Based Multi-Agent Systems.

Digital Twin Networks and Quantitative Evaluation

My earlier work explored Digital Twin Networks, sustainable in-network computing, Mobile Edge Computing, and software rejuvenation. These studies provide a foundation for evaluating the behaviour of complex software systems.
