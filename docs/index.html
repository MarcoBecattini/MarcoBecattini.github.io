About Me

I am a PhD student in Smart Computing at the University of Florence, where I am part of the Software Technologies Laboratory (STLab) in the Department of Information Engineering, under the supervision of Prof. Enrico Vicario.

My research investigates how software engineering principles and techniques can be adapted to design and govern LLM-based multi-agent systems. I focus on software architecture, agent coordination and accountability, execution control, reliability, and performance.

My work combines architectural proposals with empirical studies in industrial and educational settings. My earlier research addressed Digital Twin Networks, Mobile Edge Computing, and the quantitative evaluation of software reliability.

When I am not in front of a Mac, I am usually surfing, studying Bach, or mixing cocktails.

Research Interests

  • Software Engineering for AI (SE4AI)
  • LLM-based multi-agent systems
  • Software architecture and agent coordination
  • Reliability and performance evaluation
  • Digital Twin Networks
