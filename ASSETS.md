## Final ImageGen prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: panoramic header photograph for a personal academic computer-science website. Original editorial still-life photograph of an academic desk: open cream notebook with indistinct handwritten research notes, a black pencil, a white ceramic coffee cup partially in frame, small stack of papers on warm medium-brown wood. Natural window light, quiet thoughtful atmosphere, understated warm neutral palette, real paper grain. Wide landscape composition intended to be displayed as a very shallow panoramic strip; arrange the notebook, cup and pencil across the central horizontal band with generous surrounding desk space so a center crop still reads clearly. No people, no brands, no legible text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark. A real photograph, not an illustration or a web page mockup.
